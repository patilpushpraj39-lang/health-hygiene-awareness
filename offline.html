OUR OBJECTIVE

Make prevention
easier to understand.

Explain the relationship between safe water, sanitation, hygiene and public health in simple language. Encourage responsible personal habits and care for shared surroundings.

The original contribution discusses food and drinking water, clean hands and shared surfaces, waste and drainage, and hygienic personal surroundings. This website adds a daily checklist, an interactive guide and a practice quiz.
